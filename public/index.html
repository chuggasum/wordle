How to play

Find the hidden five-letter word in six guesses. After each guess the tiles show how close you were.

CRANE

C is in the word and in the right spot.

SPICY

P is in the word but in the wrong spot.

BLUSH

S is not in the word at all.

A new daily puzzle appears at midnight your time. Finished early? Play unlimited practice rounds, or with a word of your own.
